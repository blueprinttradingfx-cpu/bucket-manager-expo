# Bucket Portfolio Manager — Marketing & Brand Plan

**Goal:** Establish as a long-term PH investing resource/brand (not just app promo).
**Monetization:** Not decided yet — keep messaging value-first, avoid pricing claims.
**Time budget:** Moderate — daily but quick. 5 original posts/week, weekends are engagement-only.

---

## 1. Positioning

The app is built around a real insight: PH bucket/dividend investors already follow specific voices (Kuya Jon, InvestingPH, MoneyWise Engineer, Stocksilog, Trina's Snowball Picks, eTrader) and the app literally lets people import their published lists. That's the honest positioning:

> **"Bucket Portfolio Manager" isn't competing with PH investing creators — it's the tool that makes following any of them (or your own strategy) easier to track.**

**Boundary already agreed:** never brand content around a specific creator's name/picks without them knowing first ("see what Kuya Jon is buying, now in the app" — explicitly ruled out). Community Spotlight posts only go out after real outreach, with credit and their awareness.

---

## 2. Voice & Tone Guide

**Taglish, casual, friendly — "kuya/ate looking out for you," not "official brand account."**

- Financial terms stay in English (dividend, yield, REIT, buy-below price) — that's how the community actually talks. Connective/conversational language goes Tagalog or Taglish.
- Warm, direct address: "mga kabayan," "sa inyo," "tara," "diba?"
- Always end with a question or clear CTA — comments are the whole point, not just impressions.
- Emoji: light touch (1-3 per post), not decoration overload.
- Avoid: absolute promises ("sure win," "guaranteed"), hard sells, lecture-y tone, walls of text without line breaks.
- Every post involving specific tickers/prices gets a short disclaimer line — cheap insurance, and it's honest.

**Note on example numbers below:** all prices/tickers in the filled-in examples are illustrative placeholders to show the format — not real current PSE data. Swap in real numbers (from your own price-cache pipeline) when actually posting.

### 2. Bucket Method 101
*One concept per post — education, builds trust as a resource, not just a tool.*

**Template**
```
💡 Alamin natin: [Concept]

Sa simpleng salita: [1-2 sentence explanation, parang kinukwento mo sa kaibigan]

Halimbawa: [concrete PSE example]

Bakit importante 'to: [1 practical sentence]

May term ba kayong gustong ipaliwanag ko next? Comment down below! 👇
```

**Example**
```
💡 Alamin natin: Ano ba 'yang "Buy-Below Price"?

Halimbawa: Sabihin nating gusto mong bilhin ang GLO pero okay ka lang kung ₱1,600 pababa. So kapag umabot dun ang presyo, doon ka papasok — hindi mo na kailangan i-check araw-araw kung tama na ba ang timing.

Bakit importante 'to: Tinatanggal niya 'yung stress ng "bibili ba ako ngayon o hindi?" — may malinaw ka nang plano.

May term ba kayong gustong ipaliwanag ko next? Comment down below! 👇
```

## 5. Community & Market Discovery

- **r/phinvest** — ~796k members, PH-focused investing/money discussion. Highest-leverage spot for both discovery (real pain points in comment threads) and eventual launch buzz.
- **PH investing Facebook groups** — long-running ones include "Investing in the Philippines Stock Market – Tips and Tricks" and "Philippine Stock Market Discussions, Comments and Forecast." Verify current activity before engaging — membership figures found in research were dated.
- **Direct outreach**: message the creators whose portfolios are already in the app. Ask what they currently use to track their picks — real product research, and the start of a relationship for future Community Spotlight posts.
- **Comment before you post**: build presence in existing communities on pain points *before* mentioning the app — that language becomes your future post copy, for free.
- **Group etiquette**: don't post your own content directly in these groups (most disallow it, reads as spam) — that's where you comment, your own Page is where you post.

## 6. Facebook Page Setup Checklist

- Category: Finance / Product-or-Service (not Personal Blog — needed for Page Insights + CTA button)
- Created from your existing personal account (no new account needed — personal profile stays private, Page is the public identity)
- CTA button → waitlist link (App Store link once launched)
- Pinned post: honest founder intro — who you are, what you're building, why
- Cover image: real app screenshot, not a stock photo
- Bio: leads with the problem solved ("stop tracking your PSE dividends in a spreadsheet"), no pricing claims yet

## 7. Metrics That Actually Matter Right Now

Not follower count. Track:
- Comment/DM quality — are people describing real pain points?
- Waitlist conversion rate
- A running log of feature requests/objections heard in comments and DMs
